AlertFill setup

TradingView alert → order in NinjaTrader, in about a second. Setup takes ten minutes; run it on SIM first.

What AlertFill is

You already have alerts on TradingView — a strategy, an indicator, a price level. AlertFill turns those alerts into real orders in NinjaTrader. The alert fires, TradingView posts it to your personal AlertFill webhook, and the Tradeaholiks relay pushes it straight down a live connection to a small receiver running inside NinjaTrader — which places the order, stop and target attached. No polling, no browser extension, no PC-to-PC networking.

Two safety rules are built in. If your NinjaTrader is offline when an alert fires, the order queues and is delivered the moment the receiver reconnects — unless it is older than 30 seconds, in which case it is discarded and logged. A stale entry signal never fires into a market that has moved on. Second: everything is visible — every alert shows up in the receiver's activity log and on your account page.

What you'll need

NinjaTrader 8 on Windows; a paid TradingView plan (webhook alerts unlock at their Essential tier and up); and an AlertFill key — free 7-day trial or full licence, both on your account page.

Before you start: log in at tradeaholiks.com/account and open the AlertFill tab. No licence yet? Press Start my free 7-day trial — full product, no card. Everything you'll paste below has a Copy button on that tab.

Step 1 — install the receiver in NinjaTrader

1.Download TAH_AlertFill.zip from your account page's AlertFill tab (the download button under your key). Don't unzip it.

2.In NinjaTrader: Tools → Import → NinjaScript Add-On... and pick the zip. Restart NinjaTrader.

3.Open Control Center → New → TAH AlertFill. The AlertFill window opens.

4.On your account page's AlertFill tab, click Copy next to Your AlertFill key, and paste it into the window's key field.

5.Pick your account from the dropdown — it lists the accounts currently connected in NinjaTrader. Start with a SIM account.

6.Click Start. The status light goes green and your account page flips to RECEIVER CONNECTED. Tick Start automatically when NinjaTrader opens once you're happy with it.

Step 2 — point a TradingView alert at it

1.On your account page's AlertFill tab, click Copy next to TradingView webhook URL.

2.In TradingView, create an alert as usual (or edit an existing one).

3.In the alert dialog, open the Notifications tab, tick Webhook URL, and paste the copied URL.

4.In the Message box, write the order — for example: buy NQ q=1 sl=40t tp=80t

5.Save the alert. When it fires, the order appears in NinjaTrader about a second later. Watch the first few on SIM.

Writing the alert message

The message is one line: an action, a symbol, then any options in key=value form, separated by spaces.

PartWhat it does
buy  /  longMarket buy.
sell  /  shortMarket sell.
close  /  flat  /  flatten  /  exitClose the open position (and cancel its bracket orders).
NQ  or  NQ 09-26The instrument. On its own the front contract is used; add the contract month to pin one.
q=2  /  qty=2Quantity. Default 1 (or the window's default).
sl=29500  /  sl=40t  /  sl=10pStop loss: a price, ticks (40t, 40ticks) or points (10p, 10pts). Also stop=.
tp=29650  /  tp=80t  /  tp=20pTake profit, same formats. Also target=. Tick/point distances are measured from your actual fill price.
account=Sim101Send this alert to a different account than the window's selection. Also acct=.
atm=Scalp  /  atm="My ATM Name"Hand the trade to a NinjaTrader ATM strategy template. Names with spaces need the quotes.

Examples: buy NQ q=1 sl=40t tp=80t  ·  sell ES 09-26 q=2 sl=6410.25 tp=6395  ·  long NQ atm="Morning Scalp"  ·  flat NQ

TradingView placeholders work anywhere in the message — e.g. {{ticker}} or q={{strategy.order.contracts}} — TradingView fills them in before sending. JSON-style messages are also accepted ({"action":"buy","symbol":"NQ","qty":1}) if your strategy already produces them.

How ATM templates are chosen

Three levels, first one wins: 1) atm= named in the alert · 2) the ATM template currently selected in Chart Trader on that instrument's open chart · 3) the default ATM set in the AlertFill window. When an ATM runs the trade, the ATM owns the stop and target — any sl=/tp= in the alert is ignored. No ATM anywhere? AlertFill places its own bracket from sl=/tp=.

Prop-firm accounts

Most futures prop firms allow automation in some form, but each firm sets its own rules — some require you at the screen, some restrict fully unattended trading, some care only about results. Check your firm's rulebook (our PropFirm Browser is a good place to start) before running AlertFill on a funded account, and prove any strategy on SIM or an eval first. The 30-second stale rule protects you from the classic automation blow-up — a pile of delayed alerts firing into a moved market after a disconnect.

If something's off

No order arrived: check the receiver window is started (green status) and your account page says RECEIVER CONNECTED; then check the alert actually fired in TradingView (its alert log) and that the webhook URL is the full copied one — copy it again, don't retype. "ignored (couldn't parse)" in the log: the message must start with buy/sell/long/short/close — check for typos in the first word. ATM error in the log: the template name in atm= must match a saved ATM template exactly; names with spaces need quotes. Order marked discarded on the account page: it arrived more than 30 seconds old (receiver was offline) — that's the safety net doing its job. Still stuck? The contact page.
